Privacy Policy

Formal Invoicing has no account, no server and no analytics. Your invoices, clients and products stay on your device.

Applies to Formal Invoicing for iOS and Android · Last updated 13 November 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and there is no sign-up.
  • We do not run analytics, crash-tracking or attribution of our own.
  • The records you create are stored on your device. We have no copy and no way to read them.
  • On iOS, ads are served by Google AdMob. That is the only part of the app that sends data off your device.
  • The same applies on Android: Google AdMob serves the ads, and the Play Store data safety section declares the same data.

Who we are

Formal Invoicing is developed and published by Jdevio (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Formal Invoicing apps for iOS and Android and explains what the apps do with information. For any question about it, write to support@jdevio.com.

Information you create in the app

You enter business details, client records, products and services, invoices, quotations, purchase bills, payments, notes and terms. You may also add a logo, a signature and a brand colour.

This information is stored only on your device — on iOS in the app’s local database, on Android in the app’s private storage. It is not uploaded to us. We operate no server that receives it, so we cannot read, sell or share it, and we cannot recover it for you if it is lost.

Exporting a PDF or a backup creates a file through the system share sheet. Where that file goes — Files, Mail, a cloud drive, a messaging app — is your choice and is governed by that destination’s policy.

iCloud sync (iOS only)

If your device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, the app can sync its records through your private CloudKit database. That database belongs to your Apple Account: Apple stores and processes it under Apple’s Privacy Policy, and we cannot access its contents. There is no shared or developer-visible copy of your data.

To stop syncing, turn iCloud off for Formal Invoicing in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, or keep iCloud disabled on the device. The app continues to work fully offline.

Your choices. On first launch, and whenever you change the setting, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, ads are served without the advertising identifier. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google appears before any ad is requested, and Settings → Ad privacy options lets you change that choice at any time. Settings → Ads also lets you reset the advertising identifier or turn on “Limit Ad Tracking” system-wide.

Android. The Android app uses the same Google AdMob service, and the same four kinds of data apply, with two differences. Instead of the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) it uses the Google advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt on Android; instead the consent form described above is shown where the law requires it, and the same Ad privacy options entry is available in Settings. The Play Store data safety section lists Device or other IDs, App activity, Crash logs and Diagnostics for this app.

What we do not do

  • No account, email address, phone number or password is required or collected.
  • No analytics, session recording, A/B testing or attribution SDK of our own.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, calendar or location. Choosing a logo or signature image uses the system photo picker, which shares only the image you select.
  • No selling of personal information, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • No ads inside the invoice editor, the PDF preview or the share sheet on either platform.

Face ID, Touch ID and app lock

If you enable the app lock, authentication is performed by iOS. The app receives only a success or failure result. It never sees your passcode, your face data or your fingerprint, and none of that data leaves your device.

Retention and deletion

  • Your records: kept on your device until you delete them. Settings → Erase all removes everything, as does deleting the app. Deleting the app also removes its local database; if iCloud sync is on, remember to remove the app’s iCloud data in iOS Settings if you want that copy gone too.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to its own retention periods. We do not hold a copy.
  • Because we hold no personal data, there is no account or server record for us to delete on your behalf.

Security

Records are stored inside the app’s sandbox and protected by iOS and Android file-level encryption and your device passcode. iCloud data is encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest. There is no developer-operated server holding your data, so there is no central store to breach. No system is perfectly secure; keep your device passcode enabled and use the app lock for sensitive records.

Children

Formal Invoicing is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children, we do not knowingly collect information from children, and no account or profile is created for any user. If you believe a child has used the app, simply erase the data from Settings.

International users

Your business records do not leave your device, so no international transfer of that data occurs. Where ad data is processed by Google, it may be processed in countries other than yours under Google’s safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change — for example if a future version adds a paid tier or another advertising network — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the app’s release notes. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
