Common questions

Where are my invoices stored?

On your device. On iOS, records live in the app’s local database and sync through your own private iCloud if iCloud is available. On Android, records live in the app’s private storage as a single workspace file. Nothing is stored on a server we operate.

How do I move my work to a new phone?

iPhone or iPad: sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled and the workspace syncs automatically. You can also create a full JSON copy via Settings → Backup, then keep it wherever you keep important files.

Android: version 1.0 keeps everything on the device and has no export yet. Use your phone’s system backup or a manufacturer transfer tool before switching devices — and please do not uninstall the app before you have transferred your data.

Can I keep more than one business in the app?

Yes. Each business profile has its own clients, documents, catalogue, branding, currency and invoice numbering. Switch profiles from Home or Settings. Records you created before adding a second profile are moved to the first one.

How do I remove ads?

On iOS, open Settings → Support the app and watch a short video: ads are hidden for 24 hours across all of your business profiles. There is no purchase and no subscription.

The Android version (1.0) shows no ads at all.

How do I control personalised advertising?

On iOS, use Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app, or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining the tracking prompt does not remove ads; it removes the use of the advertising identifier for tracking. You can also reset the identifier in iOS Settings at any time.

The PDF shows the wrong currency, tax or totals

Currency is set per business profile in Settings → Business, and existing documents keep the currency they were created with. Tax and discount are configured per line and per document. If a total still looks wrong, check whether shipping or a document-level discount is applied — the invoice summary breaks out subtotal, discount, tax and shipping separately.

My logo or signature is not appearing on the PDF

Check Settings → Branding: a logo must be set for the active business profile, and for signatures confirm that “Include signature” is on with a signatory name. Signatures print above the footer, not in the totals block.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Data → Erase all removes every record, client, product and document from the device. Deleting the app also removes its local database. If iCloud sync is enabled, also remove the app’s iCloud data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud so the synced copy is deleted too.

Face ID or passcode unlock is not working

Confirm the lock is enabled in Settings → Security and that Face ID is allowed for the app in iOS Settings. If the device passcode changed, re-open the app once and re-enable the lock. Locking out is not permanent — the device passcode always works as a fallback.

What is different in the Android version?

Android 1.0 covers invoices, quotations, purchase bills, clients, catalogue, payments, totals, PDF sharing, multiple business profiles, search and filters. Still to come: the receivables aging report and revenue chart, app lock, JSON backup export, custom logo upload and onboarding. We would rather say this plainly than surprise you after install.

Refunds and billing

Formal Invoicing is free and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, so there is nothing to refund. If you see a charge associated with this app, it is not from us — check your Apple or Google purchase history.

Reporting a bug or requesting a feature

Email us with the steps to reproduce, your device and OS version, and your app version. Feature requests are read; the roadmap is driven by what small businesses ask for most.
